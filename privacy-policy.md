# PowerMood — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

## Summary
PowerMood does not collect, store or share any personal data. No account is required, no analytics SDKs are installed, and no information leaves your device except the network requests described below.

## Information the app does NOT collect
- No name, email, phone number or account
- No contacts, photos, location or device identifiers
- No usage analytics or crash-reporting services
- No advertising SDKs

## Network requests the app makes
- Jamendo API (api.jamendo.com): to search and stream royalty-free music in the Mood section, using a public API client ID. No user data is sent.
- radio-browser.info: to fetch the list of free radio stations and their stream URLs. No user data is sent.
- Radio stream URLs: the app connects directly to the station's stream to play audio.

## Permissions and why they are used
- **Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)**: to list and play the music stored on your device. Your library stays on the device.
- **Notifications / exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, POST_NOTIFICATIONS)**: to fire wake-up alarms at the exact time, including when the app is in the background.
- **Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)**: to show the alarm screen over the lock screen so you can stop or snooze.
- **Internet (INTERNET)**: to stream mood playlists and radio.
- **Wake lock / foreground service**: to keep audio and alarms running reliably.

## Purchases
PowerMood is free to download with a 10-day trial of Premium. After the trial, Premium is a subscription of $2.00 per month, processed by Google Play. Google handles all payment data; the app only receives a confirmation that you own the subscription. You can cancel at any time in your Google Play account settings.

## Data retention
Nothing to retain — we hold no user data.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL before the relevant feature ships.
